Hallowed Forge is real?!
Hallowed Forge is finally here. Check it out here: steam:2633870801
Once you have it be sure to change the map to Hallowed Tomb and set the waveset to Tomb Raid.

Visit the graves of your enemies in search of wonders; only to be trapped with no way out. Lucky for you, a Kind Stranger happens to be "visiting" as well.

Hallowed Forge is our first expansion to ReForged. It includes custom mobs, wavesets, equipment, and achievements. We wont spoil any more than that, so hop on a server and Forge on!

Additionally, the LavaArena map has been changed slightly to fit in with the Hallowed Theme. Check out the full patch notes below or the mod page if you want to know all of the changes that were made in this update.
